# Perihan — Elevator Pitch

**Your personal AI assistant that actually knows you.**

Perihan is a private AI assistant that learns your life — your messages, emails, documents, calendar, and projects — then helps you manage it. Not just search. *Assist.*

Today it answers questions: "When does school reopen?" "What did my lawyer say?" "What's Seb's email?" — across thousands of messages, in any language, in seconds.

Tomorrow it coaches you: reminding you of your goals, preparing you for meetings based on past conversations, helping you stay on track — all grounded in context that only *your* assistant would know.

Unlike ChatGPT, Perihan doesn't know everything on the internet. It knows everything about *you* — privately, on your own machine.

**The POC already handles 27 real-world questions across English and Turkish with 90%+ accuracy.** Document search is the foundation. The personal assistant is the product.

We're building the AI that sits between you and the chaos of your digital life — not just to search it, but to help you *live* it better.
