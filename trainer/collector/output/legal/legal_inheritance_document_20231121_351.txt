**4. Background & Purpose:**

This document outlines the proposed transfer of the property located at 14 Elmwood Drive, Westbury-on-Trym, Bristol, from the estate of the late George William Marsh to his three surviving children, in accordance with the terms of his Last Will and Testament dated 03/03/2015. The property is to be held by the beneficiaries as Tenants in Common, with each holding an equal one-third share.

The beneficiaries are:

*   Ethan Alexander Marsh (Main Party)
*   Hannah Louise Marsh (Sister, residing in Manchester)
*   Luke Thomas Marsh (Brother, residing in Edinburgh)

Probate was granted on 12/09/2022 (Probate Registry Case No. PR-2022-005678). This transfer is the final step in the distribution of the primary asset of the estate.
