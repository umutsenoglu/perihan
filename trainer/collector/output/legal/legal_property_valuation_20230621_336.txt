**4. VALUATION ASSESSMENT**

**4.1. Current Market Value (Undisputed Boundary):**
Based on the current market conditions in Highgate and analysis of comparable sales, the estimated market value of 14 Willowbrook Crescent, assuming an undisputed and clearly defined boundary line in its original position, is **£985,000 (Nine Hundred and Eighty-Five Thousand Pounds Sterling)**.

**4.2. Impact of Disputed Boundary:**
The current dispute regarding the fence line, specifically Mr. Mitchell's unilateral repositioning of the fence approximately 30cm into Mr. Marsh's garden, has several potential impacts:
*   **Loss of Amenity:** Reduction in usable garden space.
*   **Marketability:** Potential buyers may be deterred by ongoing disputes or the perceived encroachment, leading to a longer sales period or reduced offers.
*   **Legal Costs:** Associated legal and surveying costs incurred by Mr. Marsh.

**4.3. Estimated Diminution in Value:**
Should the boundary dispute not be resolved favourably for Mr. Marsh, leading to a permanent reduction in garden size, we estimate a diminution in property value of approximately **£25,000 - £40,000**. This figure accounts for the direct loss of land and the indirect impact on market perception and amenity.

**5. HISTORICAL BOUNDARY OBSERVATIONS**

Based on our review of historical documents and Mr. Marsh's personal records:
*   **Pre-2022 Fence:** The original timber panel fence, which stood until late 2022, appeared to align with the concrete post footings visible in Mr. Marsh's garden, consistent with the boundary depicted on the 1985 OS map revision.
*   **Evidence from Mr. Marsh's Records:** A photograph from Lily Marsh's 5th birthday in August 2021 clearly shows the original fence line, with a distinctive weathered post that Mr. Marsh confirms was aligned with the edge of his raised flower bed, a feature that has remained in its original position. This photograph has been provided to Ms. Crawford.
*   **Current Discrepancy:** The newly erected fence by Mr. Mitchell is observed to be approximately 30cm east of the original line, as evidenced by the historical markers and photographs.

---

**6. CONCLUSION**

The property at 14 Willowbrook Crescent, Highgate, London N6 5AB, holds a strong market value within the Highgate area. However, the ongoing boundary dispute with Mr. Dave Mitchell at 16 Willowbrook Crescent, specifically concerning the repositioning of the shared fence, poses a significant risk to this value. Our assessment indicates a potential diminution in value if the encroachment is not rectified. Historical evidence strongly suggests the original fence line was located further west than its current position.
