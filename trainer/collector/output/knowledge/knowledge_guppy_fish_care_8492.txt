**Date:** 2024-10-26

**Context:** Need to set up a small nursery tank for the inevitable fry from the main 120L. Claire's been joking about me becoming a professional fish breeder, but Lily genuinely loves seeing the little ones. Max just tries to poke the glass too hard.

**Current Thoughts/Questions:**

*   **Tank Size:** What's the absolute minimum for a temporary fry tank? Thinking a 10-20L cube. Don't want it taking up too much space, especially with the solicitors' letters about the fence still cluttering up the kitchen counter.
*   **Filtration:** Sponge filter seems simplest and safest for fry. Less suction. Need to check if I have a spare air pump somewhere in the garage, or if the old Fluval U2 for the main tank has an air stone output I could adapt.
*   **Heating:** Small heater essential. Need to aim for 24-26°C. My current heaters are all too powerful. Might have to order one online. Amazon Prime next-day delivery?
*   **Substrate/Decor:** Bare bottom or very fine sand? Bare is easier to clean, but maybe less natural. Some java moss or artificial plants for cover. Lily would probably want some colourful gravel, but practicality first.
*   **Feeding:** Fry food specifically? Or crushed flakes? Remember Janet used to crush up fish flakes for her goldfish fry when I was a kid. Simpler.
*   **Water Changes:** Small daily changes? Or every other day? Need to keep parameters stable, especially with new stock.
*   **Separation:** Should I set up a breeding trap in the main tank first, or just move pregnant females directly to the nursery? Breeding traps can be stressful.
*   **Source:** Need to re-read that forum thread on TropicalFishUK about guppy breeding. Also, check out Aquarium Co-Op on YouTube – they usually have solid, practical advice, unlike some of the more niche forums.
