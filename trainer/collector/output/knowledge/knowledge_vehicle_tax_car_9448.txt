## 📝 **GOV.UK Info: Tax Your Vehicle - My Notes & Action Items**

**Source:** [https://www.gov.uk/vehicle-tax](https://www.gov.uk/vehicle-tax)

### **Core Process:**

*   **Who:** Me (Ethan Marsh).
*   **What:** Renewing vehicle tax for the 2019 VW Golf.
*   **When:** Annually. My tax is due in January. DVLA usually sends a reminder letter a few weeks before.
*   **How to Tax (Documents I'll need):**
    1.  **Reminder Letter:** This is what I usually use. It has a reference number.
    2.  **Vehicle Log Book (V5C):** Must be in *my* name. Mine is. This would be used if I didn't have the reminder letter.
    3.  **Green 'New Keeper' Slip:** Only if I'd just bought the car. Not applicable here, but good to remember for when we get the EV.
*   **What if I don't have any documents?**
    *   Need to apply for a new log book (V5C). Can tax the vehicle at the same time.
    *   *Note to self:* Keep my V5C safe! It's in the filing cabinet with the house deeds and passports.

### **Payment Options:**

*   **Direct Debit:** This is what I currently use. It's usually quarterly or annually. I think mine is annual. Need to check my bank statements.
*   **Debit/Credit Card:** One-off payment option.
*   **Cannot pay by Direct Debit over the phone.** Good to know.

### **Important Reminders:**

*   **Must tax, even if exempt:** Even if a vehicle doesn't *cost* anything to tax (e.g., some EVs are currently exempt), you still *must* go through the process of taxing it. It's a legal obligation.
    *   *Future EV thought:* This is crucial. Even if the Ioniq 5 has £0 road tax, I'll still need to "tax" it via the DVLA.
*   **Legal obligations before driving:** Obvious, but worth noting – means valid MOT and insurance. My MOT is due Dec. Insurance is Feb. All in line.

### **Ways to Apply:**

1.  **Online (Preferred!):** Use the GOV.UK service. This is my usual method. Quick and easy.
2.  **By Phone (DVLA Vehicle Tax Service):**
    *   **Number:** 0300 123 4321
    *   **Hours:** 24-hour service.
    *   **Call Charges:** Need to check these. Usually standard rates for 0300 numbers, included in most mobile plans.
    *   *Constraint:* Cannot pay by Direct Debit over the phone. Only card. So if I want to keep my DD going, online or Post Office.
3.  **At a Post Office:**
    *   **What to take:**
        *   Payment (cash/card) OR bank details for Direct Debit.
        *   **Crucially:** My V5C (in my name) OR the green 'new keeper' slip.
        *   **Potentially:** Evidence of a valid MOT.
            *   *Example:* Screenshot of MOT history (from GOV.UK check service) or the actual MOT certificate.
            *   *Heads up:* MOT info can take up to 2 days to update. So, if I get the MOT done on a Monday, I might not be able to tax the car immediately on Monday afternoon at the Post Office. Online might be quicker if the database updates faster.

### **Northern Ireland Specifics:**

*   Not applicable to me (living in London), but good to know for general awareness or if Luke ever moves to NI with his car. They need a paper insurance cert/cover note and an original MOT certificate or TEC evidence.

### **Vehicle Off the Road (SORN):**

*   **What it means:** Registering a vehicle as 'off the road' (Statutory Off Road Notification).
*   **When:** If I'm keeping it in a garage and not driving it (e.g., if I bought the EV but kept the Golf for a few months before selling).
*   **Impact:** Don't need to tax it.
*   *Action:* Need to remember this for when we sell the Golf. If it sits on the drive for more than a few days after the EV arrives, I should probably SORN it to avoid issues.

### **Changing Tax Class (Disabled):**

*   Not applicable to me, but good to know for others. If a car was previously used by a disabled person or if I *were* disabled and taxing for the first time, I'd need to apply at a Post Office.
