**Highgate Family Practice**
**Physiotherapy & Wellbeing Consultation Notes**

**Patient:** Ethan Marsh, 38
**Date:** 2023-04-05
**Doctor:** Dr. Anand Patel
**Condition:** Stress and Anxiety

**Patient Presentation:**
Mr. Marsh, a Senior Software Engineer, presented today reporting elevated stress and anxiety for approximately three weeks, significantly impacting his daily life. He describes difficulty sleeping, often waking around 3 AM, feeling restless, irritable, and struggling to concentrate at work. He attributes this partly to an upcoming critical project deadline at his fintech company and increasing worry about his mother, Janet's, recent health issues in Bristol. While his mild asthma and lower back pain from desk work are stable, his anxiety exacerbates overall fatigue.

Dr. Patel noted symptoms consistent with Generalised Anxiety Disorder (GAD), where anxiety about multiple aspects of life becomes difficult to control and affects everyday function.

**Initial Recommendations & Management Plan:**
1.  **Talking Therapies:** Advised self-referral to NHS talking therapies (e.g., Cognitive Behavioural Therapy - CBT) for anxiety and depression, emphasizing their effectiveness in managing GAD. Information on accessing these services via the NHS website was provided.
2.  **Self-Care Strategies:** Discussed importance of maintaining routine, regular exercise (e.g., cycling, walking with Claire and the children), a balanced diet, and dedicated relaxation time (e.g., tropical fish keeping, photography).
3.  **Sleep Hygiene:** Reviewed strategies for improving sleep, including avoiding caffeine late in the day and establishing a consistent bedtime routine.
4.  **Support Network:** Encouraged open communication with his spouse, Claire, and considering reaching out to his sister Hannah or brother Luke for support regarding his mother.

**Follow-up:** Review in 4 weeks or sooner if symptoms worsen. Patient encouraged to utilize NHS online resources for GAD support.
