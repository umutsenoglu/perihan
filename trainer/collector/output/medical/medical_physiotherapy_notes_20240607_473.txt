## Patient Education & Home Exercise Program (HEP)

**Provided to Patient:** 2024-06-07 (Initial), updated on subsequent visits.
**Last Updated:** 2024-08-30 (Maintenance Program)

### Core Principles for Back Health:

1.  **Movement is Medicine:** Avoid prolonged static postures. Regularly change positions.
2.  **Good Posture:** Maintain a neutral spine, whether sitting, standing, or lifting.
3.  **Core Strength:** A strong and stable core protects your spine.
4.  **Ergonomics:** Optimise your workspace to support good posture.
5.  **Listen to Your Body:** Respect pain signals; don't push into sharp pain.
6.  **Stress Management:** Stress can exacerbate pain. Incorporate relaxation techniques.

### Initial HEP (2024-06-07) - Focus: Pain Relief & Gentle Mobility

*   **Cat-Cow Stretch:** On hands and knees, gently arch and round your back. 10 reps, 2-3 sets, daily.
*   **Pelvic Tilts (Supine):** Lying on back, flatten lower back into floor, then gently arch. 10 reps, 2-3 sets, daily.
*   **Knee-to-Chest Stretch:** Lying on back, pull one knee, then both knees, towards chest. Hold 30s, 3 reps per side, daily.
*   **Gentle Walking:** 10-15 minutes, 2-3 times per day.

### Progression HEP (Sample from 2024-07-05) - Focus: Strengthening & Stability

*   **Bird-Dog:** On hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg, keeping core stable. 10 reps per side, 2-3 sets, daily.
*   **Glute Bridge:** Lying on back, knees bent, lift hips off floor by squeezing glutes. 10-15 reps, 2-3 sets, daily.
*   **Side Plank (modified on knees):** Support body on forearm and knees, keep body in straight line. Hold 20-30s, 2-3 reps per side, daily.
*   **Plank (on elbows and toes):** Hold 30-45s, 2-3 reps, daily. (Progress from modified if needed).
*   **Wall Squats:** Back against wall, slide down to 90 degrees knee bend. Hold 30s, 3-5 reps, daily.
*   **Reverse Lunges:** 10-12 reps per side, 2 sets, daily.
*   **Continued Gentle Walking / Stationary Cycling:** Gradually increasing duration and intensity.

### Maintenance HEP (2024-08-30) - Focus: Long-term Strength & Prevention

Perform 3 times per week, 1-2 sets of 10-15 reps (unless otherwise stated).

1.  **Warm-up:** 5-10 minutes light cardio (e.g., brisk walking, cycling).
2.  **Plank:** Hold for 45-60 seconds, 2-3 repetitions.
3.  **Deadlifts (Bodyweight or light weights):** Focus on hip hinge movement, maintaining a neutral spine.
4.  **Kettlebell Swings (light kettlebell, if comfortable):** Focus on powerful hip extension.
5.  **Glute Bridge:** Focus on glute activation.
6.  **Bird-Dog:** Focus on core stability and controlled movement.
7.  **Cool-down/Stretches:** Cat-Cow, Knee-to-Chest, Hamstring stretch.

### Ergonomic Advice

*   **Office Chair:** Adjust height so feet are flat on floor (or use footrest). Ensure lower back is supported by chair's lumbar support or a cushion.
*   **Desk Height:** Elbows should be at a 90-degree angle when typing, shoulders relaxed.
*   **Monitor:** Top of the screen should be at eye level, approximately an arm's length away.
*   **Breaks:** Take a 2-3 minute standing/stretching break every 30-45 minutes.
*   **Standing Desk:** If using one, alternate between sitting and standing, typically 20-30 minutes sitting, 10-15 minutes standing.
*   **Lifting:** Bend at knees, keep back straight, lift with legs, keep load close to body.
