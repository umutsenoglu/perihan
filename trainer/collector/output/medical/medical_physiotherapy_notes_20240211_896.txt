**TREATMENT PLAN:**

**Goals (Patient-centred):**
1.  Reduce current pain levels to 2/10 or less within 4 weeks.
2.  Improve lumbar spine flexibility, particularly flexion and extension, to enable comfortable daily activities within 6 weeks.
3.  Return to cycling (commute and weekend rides) without pain within 8 weeks.
4.  Be able to comfortably play on the floor with Max and lift Lily without pain within 6 weeks.
5.  Develop a sustainable self-management routine to prevent recurrence and manage future flare-ups.

**Interventions:**

1.  **Education and Advice:**
    *   Reassurance regarding the non-serious nature of the pain. Emphasise that back pain, particularly lower back pain, is very common and usually improves within a few weeks, but can sometimes last longer or keep coming back (as per NHS guidance).
    *   Reinforce the importance of staying active and continuing with daily activities where possible. Avoid prolonged bed rest, as this can delay recovery (NHS guidance).
    *   Discuss the role of posture in aggravating his symptoms, especially during desk work and hobbies like tropical fish keeping.
    *   Review current pain management strategies (ibuprofen, heat pack). Advise to continue with ibuprofen as tolerated, and to ensure adequate hydration.
    *   Discuss ergonomic principles for his home and office desk setup. Suggest evaluating chair support, screen height, and keyboard/mouse positioning. Recommend using a lumbar support cushion if his current chair doesn't provide adequate support.
    *   Encourage regular breaks from sitting – aim for a 5-minute movement break every 30-45 minutes. This could involve standing, walking around, or performing gentle stretches.

2.  **Manual Therapy (Initial Session):**
    *   Gentle soft tissue massage to lumbar paraspinal muscles to address muscle spasm and tenderness.
    *   Mobilisation techniques to improve lumbar spine segmental movement, focusing on restricted flexion and extension.

3.  **Exercise Prescription (Home Exercise Programme - HEP):**
    *   **Focus:** Core stability, lumbar mobility, and muscle strengthening.
    *   **Frequency:** Daily, 2-3 times per day.
    *   **Duration:** 10-15 minutes per session.
    *   **Specific Exercises (demonstrated and provided with printouts/NHS App link):**
        *   **Pelvic Tilts:** Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Gently flatten lower back into floor, then arch it slightly. Repeat 10-15 times.
        *   **Knee to Chest Stretch:** Lie on back, bring one knee to chest, hold for 20-30 seconds. Repeat 3 times each leg.
        *   **Cat-Cow Stretch:** On all fours, arch back like a cat, then drop belly towards floor like a cow. Repeat 10-15 times. (Patient noted this is similar to yoga poses he's seen Claire do).
        *   **Bird-Dog:** On all fours, extend opposite arm and leg, keeping core stable. Repeat 10-12 times each side.
        *   **Gentle Lumbar Rotations (Supine):** Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Gently let knees fall to one side, then the other. Repeat 10-15 times each side.
        *   **Bridge:** Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Lift hips off floor, hold briefly, lower slowly. Repeat 10-15 times.
    *   **Instructions:** Perform exercises slowly and controlled. Stop if pain worsens. Emphasise that consistency is key.
    *   **Resources:** Provided links to NHS back pain pilates video workout and Chartered Society of Physiotherapy: video exercises for back pain. Encouraged to explore these for additional exercises once comfortable with the basics.

4.  **Activity Modification & Integration:**
    *   **Cycling:** Advise gradual return. Start with shorter distances on flat terrain. Ensure bike setup (seat height, handlebar reach) is appropriate to minimise back strain.
    *   **Tropical Fish Keeping:** Suggest kneeling or using a small stool instead of bending from the waist when cleaning tanks.
    *   **Photography/Cooking:** Advise taking frequent short breaks, changing standing position, or using a stool when standing for extended periods.
