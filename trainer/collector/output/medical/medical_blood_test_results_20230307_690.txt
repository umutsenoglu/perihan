**Why You Had a Blood Test**
As discussed, a blood test is often done to check your health, or to find out why you're having certain symptoms. In your case, Mr. Marsh, the blood test served multiple purposes:

*   **Check your general health:** Your annual health check provides a comprehensive overview of your well-being.
*   **Find out if symptoms you're having are caused by certain conditions:** While you didn't have specific new symptoms, screening for diabetes (due to family history) and checking thyroid function are part of a thorough check.
*   **Find out if you're more likely to get a condition:** Given your family history of cardiovascular disease and your previous elevated cholesterol, the lipid profile specifically helps assess your risk of developing heart conditions and stroke.
*   **Find out how well a condition is being treated or managed:** While not for a specific condition in your case, it helps monitor your overall health trajectory and the effectiveness of your lifestyle choices.

**Types of Blood Tests You Had**
You had several types of blood tests, tailored to your routine check-up and specific concerns:

*   **Full Blood Count (FBC):** This measures the number of blood cells in your blood to check for blood conditions such as iron deficiency anaemia or infection. Your results were normal.
*   **Lipid Profile:** This measures your cholesterol levels (Total, HDL, LDL, Triglycerides) to check your risk of getting conditions like cardiovascular disease and stroke. This was a key part of your test due to your previous elevated levels and family history. Your results show elevated Total and LDL cholesterol, indicating increased risk.
*   **Blood Glucose:** This measures your blood sugar levels to check for or manage diabetes. Your fasting glucose was normal.
*   **Urea and Electrolytes (U&E), or Renal Panel:** This checks if the amount of certain substances in your blood may be causing your symptoms and, more broadly, assesses kidney function. Your results were normal, indicating healthy kidneys.
*   **Total Protein Test & Liver Function Tests (LFTs):** These measure protein and enzyme levels in your blood to check for problems with your liver or kidneys. Your results were normal, indicating healthy liver function.
*   **Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH):** This measures thyroid hormones to check for conditions like underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism). Your results were normal, indicating healthy thyroid function.
