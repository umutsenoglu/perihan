**Ethan's Kitchen Notes - 2024-01-18**

**"Speedy" Coq au Vin (sort of) - Rainy Sunday Edition**

Still raining. Proper Sunday mood. Claire and I just watched *Past Lives* – really got to me, she had a little cry. Kids are surprisingly quiet upstairs, which usually means Max is attempting to draw
on Lily, or the walls. Silence is usually a precursor to some kind of 'art project'. Claire's still a bit misty-eyed, so comfort food is essential.

This "Speedy" Coq au Vin is really just chicken thighs, bacon lardons (next time, *proper* bacon, these were a bit pathetic), shallots, and mushrooms, all bunged into the big Le Creuset. Used the Merlot James brought over last week – actually pretty decent for cooking. Just a quick sear on the chicken, then everything else in, simmered with some chicken stock and a few sprigs of thyme from the garden. No lengthy marinating, no faff. The point is "speedy," after all.

Smells pretty good bubbling away on the
stove. Decent. Claire's still on the sofa, scrolling through something, probably school stuff or looking at pictures of Labradors again. She was proper misty-eyed about the film. I'm thinking maybe some crusty bread to mop up the sauce, or perhaps just mash – Max always demolishes mash.

Lily and Max were a bit *too* quiet for a while there. Now I can hear something about a 'missing unicorn horn' and a 'purple crayon incident'. The calm is definitely over. Claire was just saying yesterday how a dog would be great for "distracting them from each other." Easy for her to say, she's not the one who'd be walking it in this weather. Though, a Labrador would love this kind of rain, wouldn't it? Mum keeps sending me links to local rescues in Bristol, saying "just looking, dear," but I know what she's up to. Will need to stir this properly soon and check for seasoning. A little redcurrant jelly might perk it up if it's missing something. Right, better go sort out the unicorn horn crisis before it escalates into glitter bombs.
