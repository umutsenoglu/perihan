**Recipe Notes: Chicken & Chorizo Pasta Bake (Attempt 2)**

Okay, trying Dad's old pasta bake recipe again, with my own tweaks. Found the handwritten card tucked into that 'Good Housekeeping' cookbook when I was boxing up some of his stuff last month. Still feels surreal, that whole inheritance process. Solicitor said we should get an update on the Bristol house probate next week, fingers crossed.

**Ingredients:**
*   400g chicken thighs, diced (was going to do breast, but thighs have more flavour)
*   150g chorizo, sliced (might dice half for better distribution)
*   1 large onion, 3 cloves garlic, 1 red pepper (chop finely, Max hates big chunks)
*   2 tins chopped tomatoes
*   1 tbsp tomato paste (for depth)
*   300g pasta (penne or fusilli – whatever's in the cupboard)
*   Mozzarella & mature cheddar for topping.

**Method notes:**
1.  Fry chorizo hard first to render out that oil. That’s where the flavour is. Drain some of the excess if it’s too much. Claire said the last one was a bit greasy.
2.  Sauté onion, pepper, garlic in the chorizo oil. Add chicken, brown it off.
3.  Stir in tomato paste, then chopped tomatoes. Simmer for 15-20 mins. Add a splash of stock maybe? Or red wine if Claire hasn't got to it.
4.  Boil pasta al dente. Drain, mix with sauce.
5.  Into oven dish, top with cheese. Bake 20-25 mins at 180°C.
