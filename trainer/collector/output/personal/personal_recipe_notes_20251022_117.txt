*   **Ingredients Checklist for Tagine:**
    *   Lamb shoulder, diced (need about 1kg) – *Butcher on Saturday? Or Tesco online delivery, but quality…*
    *   Tinned chopped tomatoes (2 cans)
    *   Chickpeas (1 can, drained)
    *   Dried apricots (approx 100g)
    *   Preserved lemons (2-3 halves, chopped) – *Check cupboard, think we have some from that chicken dish in August.*
    *   Onions (2 large)
    *   Garlic (4-5 cloves)
    *   Ginger (fresh, thumb-sized piece)
    *   Ras el hanout (2 tbsp) – *Crucial. Running low, add to shop list.*
    *   Ground cumin, coriander, turmeric (1 tsp each)
    *   Paprika (smoked, 1 tsp)
    *   Saffron (pinch) – *Optional, but good if we have it. Probably not.*
    *   Chicken stock (500ml)
    *   Couscous/Rice for serving
    *   Fresh coriander/parsley for garnish

*   **Method (Mental rehearsal):**
    1.  Brown lamb in batches. Set aside.
    2.  Sauté onions, garlic, ginger.
    3.  Add spices, toast for a minute.
    4.  Return lamb, add tomatoes, chickpeas, apricots, preserved lemon, stock. Bring to simmer.
    5.  Cover tightly. Oven 160C for 2.5-3 hours until tender.
    6.  Check seasoning, serve with couscous.
