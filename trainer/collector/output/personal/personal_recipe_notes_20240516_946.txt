*   Spices: Turmeric (1 tsp), Cumin (1 tsp), Coriander (1.5 tsp), Garam Masala (0.5 tsp) – *maybe a pinch of mild chilli powder, but just a tiny bit for flavour, not heat. Don't want Lily complaining again.*
*   Chopped tomatoes – 1 tin (400g)
*   Coconut milk – 1 tin (400ml), full fat for creaminess
*   Fresh spinach – 200g
*   Salt & Pepper to taste
*   Coriander leaves, fresh – for garnish (optional, Max will just pick it out)
*   Basmati rice – for serving

**Method:**
1.  Heat oil/ghee in a large pan over medium heat. Add onion, cook until softened and translucent, about 5-7 mins. Try not to check the phone every 30 seconds for EV updates.
2.  Add garlic and ginger, cook for another minute until fragrant. Don't burn it!
3.  Stir in the powdered spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, *tiny bit of chilli*). Cook for a minute, stirring constantly, until fragrant. This is where the magic happens.
