**Book Notes: Random thoughts on 'The Organized Mind' (or something like it)**

Date: 2023-11-18

"The brain is for having ideas, not holding them." – This line keeps hitting me. It’s so simple, yet I feel like my head is constantly a hard drive at 98% capacity, juggling processes in the background. My own internal task manager is a mess.

Had a really long call with Mum today, in Bristol. She sounded so tired. Just… depleted. George passing almost two years ago still weighs so heavily on her, I think. She said she'd just been "pottering," which is her code for not doing much. Asked if Hannah had called her this week, then remembered Hannah was up to her eyes with that big presentation. Need to check in with Hannah too. Mum promised she’d rest up. Hope she does. I should ring Luke tomorrow, see if he’s heard from her.

Back to the book stuff – the idea of offloading decisions. God, I need to apply that to the fence. This whole neighbor dispute with the Williams is killing me. The solicitor, Simon, sent another email this morning, outlining the next steps for mediation. It’s so much back and forth, legal jargon, and frankly, outright pettiness from their side about a boundary line that's been there for thirty years. Every email feels like a punch to the gut. I just want it resolved. Claire says I look stressed, she's right. It's constant background noise.

Richard just slack-ed me about the quarterly review docs. It’s relentless. Sometimes I just want to switch off my phone and stare at the fish tank for an hour. They don't have KPIs or solicitors.

Speaking of switching off, I need to get back on the bike. Haven't been out properly since last weekend because of the rain and then just... exhaustion. My lower back has been playing up again, probably from slumping over the desk too much. Need to remember to do those stretches Claire found for me. Maybe a quick spin tomorrow morning before everyone else is up, if Max doesn’t decide 5 AM is playtime.

Claire mentioned that Barclays sent a letter about mortgage rates. Just what we need. Another thing to think about. We're fixed for another year, but still, the constant reminders of expenses. And the car – the Golf is fine, but it’s getting on a bit, and I keep seeing these electric ones. The environmental side is a big draw, plus the fuel savings. But then, it's a huge outlay. Another big decision hanging over my head.

Cooked that new chicken and chorizo paella recipe last night. It was actually really good, Lily even had seconds, which is a miracle. Max mostly picked out the chicken. Claire loved it, which made the effort worth it. Small wins.

This book really is making me think. About simplifying, about letting go of things that aren't mine to carry. But how do you just 'let go' of a fence dispute when it directly impacts your home and your peace of mind? Or let go of the worry about Mum, or the pressure at work? Maybe it’s not about letting go entirely, but about finding a better way to hold it. Or like the book says, building a better system for the 'hard drive' that is my brain. Probably needs a defrag.

Anyway. Time to log off. Need to check Lily’s reading book with her and then put Max to bed. Another day, another set of tasks for the internal manager.
