**03.06.2024**

Monday. Long one. Another sprint planning session that went nowhere, felt like Richard was just arguing for the sake of it. Claire had a rough day with her Year 2s – apparently, a "major glitter incident" that she's still finding specs of on her. Lily was buzzing about Mrs. Henderson saying her drawing was "very imaginative," and Max somehow managed to smear banana on the ceiling at nursery. Just typical Monday chaos.

Got home and just wanted to crash, but Claire immediately launched into kitchen stuff. She's right, we need to get this sorted. That dodgy tap is driving me mad, and the oven door barely closes. Had another quote come through from 'Modern Kitchens Co.' – 22k! Seriously? That's after the 'Premium Homes Design' one came in at 25.5k. Starting to think my back-of-a-napkin calculations were wildly optimistic. This is exactly why I've been pushing to sell Dad's house in Bristol. Needed that capital.

And that's where my mind went, as it usually does these days. Luke. Just thinking about how… quiet things have gotten between us. Used to speak to him almost every week, always had our Sunday chess games on Chess.com. Now? Nothing. It’s been months. Since that blow-up on 12 January. God, that phone call still stings. He just wouldn't listen. Accused me of "only caring about money" when all I was trying to do was explain about the *actual* costs of living, of getting this kitchen done, of future proofing things for the kids. He hung up on me. On his own brother.

Still can’t believe he thinks keeping Dad’s place as some kind of 'memorial' is a sensible financial plan. Dad would’ve wanted us to make smart choices, not just cling to sentimentality. I know he took Dad’s death really hard – the guilt about the hiking trip, not being there in 2021… I get it, honestly. But he’s letting it cloud everything.

Hannah, bless her, tried her best to mediate. That whole compromise on 3 February, to just keep the house for another year and "revisit" it then. It felt like kicking the can down the road, but I went along with it for Mum's sake. Janet's been so upset, apparently telling Hannah in tears how much it hurts her that we're fighting. That's the last thing Dad would have wanted.

Luke did send that brief "sorry for what I said" text on 8 February. And I replied "it's fine." But it’s not fine. Not really. Every conversation since then feels… strained. Like walking on eggshells. I miss the easy banter, the ridiculous chess taunts. He was always closest to Dad, and maybe that's why this is so hard for him. But I just wish he’d see things from someone else’s perspective. It makes me sad, actually. Really sad.

Got to remember to cycle tomorrow morning, clear my head. Back's been a bit twingy again. Maybe the fish need a new plant too. Distractions. Always distractions.
