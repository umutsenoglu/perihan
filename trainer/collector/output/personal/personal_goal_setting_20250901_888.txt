September 1st, 2025 – Monday

Ugh. Monday. And not just any Monday. Woke up feeling like someone had replaced my lower spine with a particularly rigid piece of timber. Left side, just above the hip, burning. Every time I shift, a sharp twinge. Great start to the week. Max, of course, chose today to demand a full rendition of "Old MacDonald" at 6:15 AM, followed by a tantrum because his toast was "too crunchy." Bless him.

And then there's the nursery letter. It was waiting on the kitchen counter when I came down for coffee yesterday morning, innocently tucked under a pile of junk mail. Claire had opened it already. The fees for Little Stars are going up. Again. By nearly 15%. Fifteen. Percent. For a part-time spot. My heart actually did a little flop. Claire just looked at me, weary, holding the kettle. "We need to talk about this," she said. No kidding.

It's been nagging at me all weekend, even more than the dull ache that was already starting in my back. Lily’s Year 3 fees for QPA were already a stretch, what with the 'voluntary' contributions and the endless list of art supplies. Max starting reception next year feels like light years away, but these nursery costs are unsustainable. It's like we're constantly running just to stay in the same place.

This back pain... it's a proper flare-up this time. Not just a niggle. I can barely sit at my desk, which is a problem when your job description literally involves sitting at a desk for 8 hours a day. My posture must be absolutely shot. I need to book that physio appointment. Claire’s been nagging me for weeks. Said I should go to Mark, the one she saw after her knee thing a few years back. He's pricey, but apparently good.

Right. This isn’t a complaint session. This is a "get my head straight" session. I need to get organised, actually put down some thoughts, some goals. Stop drifting. This year is flying by already. It’s September 1st, for crying out loud. Christmas decorations will be in Sainsbury’s next week.

Okay, deep breath.

**Goals & Action Plan – September 2025**

**I. Health & Well-being (Back Pain Priority)**

*   **Problem:** Chronic lower back pain, currently flaring up significantly. Affecting work, sleep, hobbies. Mild asthma, seasonal allergies (pollen still bad sometimes).
*   **Goal:** Reduce back pain to manageable levels; improve overall posture and core strength. Maintain general health.
*   **Actions:**
    *   [ ] **Book Physio ASAP:** Call Mark’s clinic (Claire's physio) before end of today. Get first appointment scheduled this week. *Self-note: Don't put this off. It's impacting everything.*
    *   [ ] **Daily Stretches/Mobility:** Implement 10-15 minutes of specific lower back stretches (e.g., Cat-Cow, Child's Pose, Piriformis stretch) every morning and evening. Set phone reminder.
    *   [ ] **Ergonomics Check:** Re-evaluate desk setup at work and home.
        *   Adjust chair height, monitor position.
        *   Try standing desk more often at work (even if it hurts initially, need to build tolerance).
        *   Maybe get a lumbar support cushion for the car.
    *   [ ] **Core Strengthening:** Once physio approves, start gentle core exercises (e.g., planks, bird-dog) 3x week.
    *   [ ] **Cycling Posture Review:** Check bike fit. Could my road bike position be contributing? Maybe too aggressive.
