2023-04-11

Tuesday. What a day. Kicked off with that cycling ride with Marcus, bloody brilliant. Hampstead Heath was looking stunning, still a bit chilly in the shade but the sun felt proper warm when we hit the open bits near Kenwood House. Marcus was complaining about his gears, as usual, but the climb up the hill felt good, got the heart going. We were coming back down towards the ponds when we saw it – a fox, just trotting across the path, bold as brass. Not even a quick dash, just sauntering along, bushy tail waving. Must’ve been looking for breakfast. We just stopped, totally still, and watched it disappear into the bushes. Moments like that make the early start worth it. Felt really refreshed afterwards, despite the usual post-ride ache in my lower back – really need to remember those stretches. Claire just rolls her eyes when I complain, says I need to invest in a better chair at work if it's that bad.

Got back, showered, quick check on the fish tank – levels all good, no dramas. Then the usual morning madness of getting Lily and Max out the door. Lily was buzzing about some science experiment Mrs. Henderson is doing this week at QPA Primary. Max, meanwhile, was mostly interested in whether his dinosaur toy could come to Little Stars Nursery with him. The usual negotiations. Claire was already halfway out the door for school, reminding me about Portugal.

Ah, Portugal. The ongoing saga. The flight situation is driving me a bit mad. I swear the prices went up overnight again. Claire's set on the place near Lagos, and it looks amazing for the kids, but those flights… need to sit down properly tonight and just get them booked. Before they go up another £100. Feel like I’ve been saying "I'll do it tonight" for a week.
