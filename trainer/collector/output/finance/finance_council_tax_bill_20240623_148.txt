**What happens if you don't pay?**

If you do not pay your Council Tax on time, you will be sent a reminder notice. If you fail to pay following a reminder, you may lose your right to pay by instalments and the full outstanding balance for the year will become due immediately. Failure to pay will result in further recovery action, which could include court summons and enforcement agent action. We understand that financial difficulties can arise; if you are struggling to pay, please contact us as soon as possible to discuss your options.
