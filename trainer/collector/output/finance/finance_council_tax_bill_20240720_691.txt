**Council Tax Reductions and Support:**

We offer various forms of support for residents who may be struggling to pay their Council Tax.

*   **Council Tax Support (CTS):** If you are on a low income or receive certain benefits, you may be eligible for Council Tax Support. This scheme can reduce the amount of Council Tax you have to pay. We encourage you to check your eligibility, especially if your household income has changed (e.g., if Claire's teaching hours were to be reduced, or if your company had a difficult quarter).
*   **Disability Reduction Scheme:** If you or someone living with you is permanently disabled, and your home has been adapted to meet their needs (e.g., extra bathroom, kitchen, or space for a wheelchair), you may be eligible for a reduction in your Council Tax bill. This means your bill will be calculated as if your property was in the band immediately below its actual band. For example, if your property is Band D, your bill would be charged at the Band C rate.
*   **Second Adult Rebate:** If you live alone or with only one other adult (who is not your spouse or partner) who is on a low income, you may be eligible for a Second Adult Rebate.
*   **Exemptions:** Certain properties or individuals may be exempt from Council Tax entirely (e.g., properties solely occupied by students, severely mentally impaired persons, or those under 18).

For more information on these schemes and to apply, please visit haringey.gov.uk/counciltaxsupport or call our Customer Services team.
