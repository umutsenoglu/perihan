**2.1. Buildings Insurance**

Your Buildings Insurance covers the structure of your home, including its permanent fixtures and fittings, against a range of specified perils. The sum insured of £550,000 is based on the estimated cost to rebuild your home from the ground up, not its market value. We review this figure annually using industry-standard indices to help ensure you are adequately covered against rising construction costs.

**Key Features:**

*   **Rebuild Cost Cover:** Up to £550,000 for the rebuilding of your property, including outbuildings (shed, garage), walls, gates, fences, drives, paths, patios, and fixed domestic fuel tanks, swimming pools, and tennis courts.
*   **Accidental Damage:** Cover for unforeseen and unintended damage to your buildings, such as drilling through a pipe or accidentally dropping something heavy on a ceramic hob.
*   **Subsidence, Heave, or Landslip:** Cover for damage caused by the movement of the ground beneath your home. This is a crucial cover, especially in areas with varying soil types or historical mining activity.
*   **Escape of Water or Oil:** Protection against damage caused by burst pipes, leaking appliances (e.g., washing machine, dishwasher), or overflowing tanks. This is particularly relevant for modern homes with complex plumbing systems.
*   **Fire, Lightning, Explosion, Earthquake, Aircraft:** Comprehensive cover for damage from these catastrophic events.
*   **Storm and Flood:** Protection against damage caused by severe weather conditions, including high winds, heavy rain, hail, and flooding from external sources.
*   **Theft or Attempted Theft:** Cover for damage to the building resulting from a break-in or attempted break-in.
*   **Malicious Damage or Vandalism:** Cover for intentional damage caused by third parties.
*   **Impact by Vehicles or Animals:** Cover for damage caused by a vehicle crashing into your home or by animals (e.g., deer, livestock).
*   **Falling Trees, Aerials, or Satellite Dishes:** Cover for damage caused by these falling objects.
*   **Riot and Civil Commotion:** Protection against damage during public disturbances.
*   **Alternative Accommodation:** If your home becomes uninhabitable due to an insured event, we will cover the cost of alternative accommodation for your family (including Lily and Max) and pets, up to £25,000, for up to two years. This also includes loss of rent if you were letting out part of your property.
*   **Property Owner's Liability:** Up to £5,000,000 for claims made against you as the property owner for accidental injury to third parties or damage to their property arising from your ownership of the home. This includes incidents such as a loose roof tile falling and injuring a passer-by, or a tree from your garden damaging a neighbour's fence.
*   **Mortgagee Interest Clause:** Barclays Bank UK PLC, as your mortgage provider, is noted as having an interest in the property. In the event of a significant claim, Aviva will liaise directly with Barclays regarding the settlement of funds to ensure their interest is protected.

**Your Excess:** A standard excess of **£250** applies to each claim under Buildings Insurance. For subsidence, heave, or landslip claims, a higher excess of £1,000 applies.

**7.2. General Exclusions (What is Not Covered)**

*   **Wear and Tear:** Damage caused by gradual deterioration, wear and tear, or lack of maintenance is not covered. For example, a roof leaking due to old, perished felt would not be covered, but a roof damaged by a storm would be.
*   **Faulty Workmanship/Design:** Damage resulting from faulty design, defective materials, or poor workmanship is generally excluded, although subsequent damage caused by an insured peril (e.g., fire from faulty wiring) may be covered.
*   **Unoccupied Property:** Your policy has specific conditions if your home is left unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days. Please check your policy booklet for details.
*   **War & Terrorism:** Damage caused by acts of war, invasion, or terrorism (unless specifically endorsed).
*   **Nuclear Risks:** Damage caused by ionising radiations or contamination by radioactivity.
*   **Pollution & Contamination:** Damage caused by pollution or contamination, unless it results from an insured peril.
*   **Electronic Data:** Loss or corruption of electronic data (other than as covered by Family Cyber Cover).
*   **Business Use:** Use of your home for business purposes other than incidental office work, without prior declaration and agreement.
*   **Specific Event Excesses:** Certain perils, such as subsidence, heave, or landslip, may have a higher specific excess.
*   **Unspecified Items:** Individual items exceeding the single article limit (e.g., £1,000 for unspecified contents, £500 for single items under Personal Possessions worldwide) will not be covered for their full value unless specifically declared.
*   **Pests:** Damage caused by insects, birds, or vermin (other than as covered by Home Emergency for pest removal).
*   **Consequential Loss:** Loss or damage that does not directly result from the insured event (e.g., loss of earnings if you can't work from home due to damage).

*   **Buildings:** The structure of your home including its permanent fixtures and fittings, garages, sheds, walls, fences, gates, drives, paths, patios, and fixed domestic fuel tanks, swimming pools, and tennis courts.
*   **Contents:** Household goods and personal belongings within your home (including those belonging to Lily and Max), which are not part of the structure of the building. This includes furniture, electrical items, clothing, and valuables.
*   **Sum Insured:** The maximum amount we will pay for any claim under a specific section of your policy. For buildings, this is the rebuild cost; for contents, it is the replacement cost.
*   **Excess:** The first amount of any claim that you must pay yourself. For example, if you have a £250 excess and a claim is settled for £1,000, we will pay £750, and you will pay £250.
*   **New for Old:** Means that if your contents are damaged beyond repair or stolen, we will replace them with new items of similar specification, or provide a cash settlement sufficient to buy new equivalent items, without deduction for wear and tear.
*   **Accidental Damage:** Sudden and unforeseen physical damage to your property or contents that is not deliberate.
*   **Peril:** An event or cause of loss, such as fire, flood, storm, or theft, against which your policy provides cover.
*   **Material Fact:** Any information that would influence an insurer's decision to accept a risk, set the terms, or calculate the premium. You must disclose all material facts.
*   **No Claims Discount (NCD):** A discount applied to your premium based on the number of consecutive years you have not made a claim that impacted your NCD.
