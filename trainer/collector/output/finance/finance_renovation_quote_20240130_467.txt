**1. Summary of Proposed Works:**

The renovation project for your kitchen at [MASKED STREET NUMBER], [MASKED STREET NAME] will include, but is not limited to, the following key elements:

*   **Demolition & Preparation:**
    *   Removal of existing kitchen units, worktops, tiles, and appliances.
    *   Disposal of all waste materials in compliance with environmental regulations.
    *   Minor wall adjustments (non-structural opening between kitchen and dining space, subject to site survey confirmation).
    *   Preparation of walls for plastering/tiling.
    *   Preparation of floor for new flooring installation.
*   **Cabinetry & Worktops:**
    *   Supply and installation of new kitchen base and wall units (Howdens 'Clerkenwell' range, Shaker style, Dust Grey finish).
    *   Supply and installation of bespoke tall larder unit and integrated fridge-freezer housing.
    *   Supply and installation of 20mm Quartz worktops (Caesarstone 'Osprey' finish), including upstands.
    *   Installation of integrated sink (Franke Kubus KBG 110-50 Fragranite Onyx) and mixer tap (Franke Maris Pull-Out Mixer Tap).
*   **Appliances:**
    *   Installation of client-supplied appliances (integrated dishwasher, induction hob, oven, extractor hood, fridge-freezer).
    *   No appliance supply included in this quote, as per discussion.
*   **Electrics:**
    *   Re-routing and installation of new sockets (double sockets with USB ports where specified).
    *   Installation of new under-cabinet LED lighting.
    *   Installation of new ceiling spotlights (6 units, dimmable).
    *   Installation of dedicated circuits for oven and induction hob.
    *   Certification by a NICEIC approved electrician.
*   **Plumbing:**
    *   Re-routing of water supply for new sink location.
    *   Installation of dishwasher connection.
    *   Installation of washing machine connection (utility area adjacent to kitchen).
    *   Installation of new radiator (vertical anthracite, 1600x400mm) in kitchen area.
    *   All new pipework certified by a Gas Safe registered engineer for any gas connections (hob connection only if applicable, currently electric).
*   **Tiling & Flooring:**
    *   Wall tiling: Supply and installation of Metro tiles (white, 10x20cm) in brick-bond pattern, with light grey grout, for splashback areas.
    *   Floor tiling: Supply and installation of porcelain floor tiles (60x60cm, concrete effect) with underfloor heating (electric mat system).
*   **Decorating:**
    *   Plastering of walls and ceiling as required.
    *   Painting of walls and ceiling (2 coats, client-specified paint colour - e.g., Dulux Trade 'White Mist').
    *   Architrave and skirting board installation/painting.
*   **Project Management & Site Supervision:**
    *   Full project management, regular updates, and site supervision throughout the renovation.
