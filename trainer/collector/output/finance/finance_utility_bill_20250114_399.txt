**Helpful Tips for Managing Your Energy**

*   **Thermostat Settings:** Consider reducing your thermostat by just 1 degree. For example, if you usually have it at 21°C, try 20°C. This can save you around £100 a year. We see your typical setting is 20.5°C when the family is home, perhaps try 19.5°C after the kids are in bed?
*   **Draft Proofing:** Check for drafts around windows and doors. Even small gaps can let a lot of heat out. We know you're quite handy with DIY, Ethan – a quick check could make a big difference.
*   **Appliance Use:** When using your washing machine or dishwasher, try to run them on a full load and at a lower temperature setting. Claire mentioned she's been trying to do more laundry in the evenings – remember to check your tariff for off-peak rates if you have an economy 7 meter.
*   **Boiler Service:** Regular boiler servicing ensures it runs efficiently and safely. Your last service was in March 2023. We recommend an annual check, especially with the kids' asthma.
*   **Smart Meter:** You're already benefiting from a smart meter, providing accurate bills and real-time usage data. Keep an eye on your in-home display for daily cost tracking.
