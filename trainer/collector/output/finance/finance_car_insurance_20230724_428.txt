**COVER SUMMARY & EXCESSES**

This section outlines the main features of your Comprehensive Car Insurance policy. For full terms and conditions, please refer to your Policy Wording document, available at www.directline.com/policy-wording or upon request.

**Cover Type: Comprehensive**
This is our highest level of cover and includes:

*   **Accidental Damage:** Covers damage to your car even if it's your fault.
*   **Fire & Theft:** Covers loss or damage due to fire, lightning, explosion, theft or attempted theft.
*   **Third Party Liability:** Covers damage to other people's property or injury to others caused by your car.
*   **New Car Replacement:** If your car is less than 12 months old and written off, we'll replace it with a new one of the same make and model. (Your VW Golf is now 4 years old, so this benefit no longer applies).
*   **Uninsured Driver Promise:** If an uninsured driver hits your car, and it wasn’t your fault, your No Claims Discount won't be affected, and you won't have to pay your excess.
*   **Courtesy Car:** We provide a courtesy car for the duration of approved repairs if you use our approved repair network.
*   **Windscreen Cover:** Repair or replacement of your windscreen or windows. An excess of £75 applies for repairs, £115 for replacement.
*   **Personal Belongings:** Covers loss or damage to personal belongings in your car up to £250 (excluding money, credit cards, phones, and items permanently fixed to the car). This might be useful if Lily leaves her favourite cuddly toy in the car during school drop-offs and it gets damaged, for example.
*   **Medical Expenses:** Up to £100 per person for immediate medical treatment following an accident.
