**DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF WORKS COMPLETED**

The comprehensive renovation of your kitchen at Elmwood Drive included the following services and materials, as outlined in our original quotation BR-Q-2025-0147:

**1. Kitchen Cabinetry & Installation (Howdens Kitchens)**
*   Supply and installation of full range of Howdens Clerkenwell Super Matt Grey units (base, wall, and larder units).
*   Includes all necessary plinths, cornices, end panels, and soft-close mechanisms.
*   Modification of existing utility cupboard to integrate new appliance.
*   Supply and installation of new internal shelving and storage solutions.
*   Labour for assembly and fitting of all units.

**2. Worktops (Quartz)**
*   Supply and installation of 30mm Compac Alaska White quartz worktops, including upstands.
*   Cut-outs for sink and hob, with polished edges.
*   Template and fitting service included.
*   Note: As discussed with Mrs. Marsh, the island feature was not feasible due to space constraints, avoiding the need for dining area extension.

**3. Plumbing Works**
*   Isolation and removal of existing sink and dishwasher.
*   New pipework for relocation of dishwasher to integrated unit position.
*   Installation of new sink and mixer tap (customer supplied).
*   Connection of new integrated washing machine (customer supplied).
*   Testing and commissioning of all new plumbing.

**4. Electrical Works**
*   Isolation and removal of existing sockets and wiring.
*   Installation of four (4) new double power sockets for additional appliance access and countertop use.
*   Rewiring for new integrated oven and hob positions.
*   Installation of new under-cabinet LED lighting system.
*   Relocation of existing ceiling light fitting.
*   All works certified to Part P Building Regulations by qualified electrician.

**5. Tiling**
*   Preparation of wall surfaces for tiling.
*   Supply and installation of Metro style ceramic wall tiles (customer selected, sourced from Topps Tiles) for splashback areas.
*   Grouting and sealing.

**6. Painting & Decorating**
*   Preparation of walls and ceiling (filling, sanding).
*   Application of two coats of emulsion paint to walls and ceiling (Farrow & Ball 'Elephant's Breath' – customer specified).
*   Application of satinwood paint to all skirting boards and door frames.

**7. Waste Removal**
*   Removal of all old kitchen units, worktops, tiles, and associated building waste.
*   Site cleared and left tidy upon completion.
